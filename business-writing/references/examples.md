# Anonymized Examples

Use these examples only when they help determine the right level of editing, structure, tone, or data presentation.

They illustrate principles rather than fixed templates.

---

## Example 1 — Internal explanation: keep shared context

### Situation

A colleague asks why a new product configuration exists and how it differs from the old one.

### Rough draft

The old 4 toppings product was built with menu steps because we could not limit 4 toppings.
Website customers were getting confused.
We then created a topping limit feature.
I created the new product on PRE and they tested it.
Now we are piloting it and default ingredients are not printed.

### Improved

Hi T,

- The original BYO - 4 Toppings was built with menu steps because we couldn't limit customers to 4 toppings.
- This caused confusion on the website.
- We later developed the topping-limit feature, so I created a new BYO - 4 Toppings for Website/App.
- We tested it in PRE and then launched it as a limited pilot.
- During the pilot, they noticed that the default ingredients are not printed because they are configured as defaults.

My view:
- Technically, the product is working as expected.
- The impact is mainly operational, as stores are used to seeing those ingredients printed.

Best,
[Name]

### Why

The recipient already knows the product and context. The edit improves logical sequence without adding background they do not need.

## Example 2 — Decision email: surface the choice

### Situation

A temporary internet connection costs another month of service even though it is needed for only 2 days.

### Rough draft

The new internet will be installed on October 2.
I need to cancel the temporary internet today.
If I cancel it there will be no cameras or internet for 2 days.
Otherwise it costs €25.

What do you want?

### Improved

Hi all,

The new fibre connection is scheduled to be activated on October 2.

I need to cancel our temporary internet service today. We have 2 options:

1. Cancel today — the building will have no internet or camera access until the new connection is activated.
2. Keep the temporary service for another month — additional cost: €25.

Please let me know which option you prefer, as I need to make the cancellation today.

Best,
[Name]

### Why

The recipient needs to make a decision. The message foregrounds the choice, consequence, and deadline.

## Example 3 — Slack message: direct and operational

### Situation

A store has complained about service and the support team should give its tickets additional attention for a week.

### Rough draft

Any person on shift please add eyes if you read this.

The store is complaining about our service. We need to change their perspective. If any ticket comes in next 7 days give it some extra love.

### Improved

🚨 [INFO] — If you're on shift, please react with 👀 once you've read this.

The store has shared that they're unhappy with the service they've received from us. Let's work together to change that experience.

For the next 7 days, if you see a ticket from this store, please give it some extra attention: be proactive, communicate clearly, and make sure the customer feels supported throughout.

### Why

The message keeps the original energy but translates "extra love" into concrete behaviors.

## Example 4 — Spanish handover: organize ownership

### Situation

The sender is leaving a project and wants to document the remaining actions.

### Rough draft

Quería enviarles un correo antes para asegurarme que tenemos en escrito los temas abierto y los objetivos para los que vamos a seguir trabajando. Esta vez sin mi en el medio, pero con la ayuda de la nueva responsable.

### Improved

Hola a todos,

Quería enviar este correo para dejar por escrito los temas que siguen abiertos y los próximos pasos acordados.

Aunque a partir de ahora ya no estaré involucrado directamente en el proyecto, estoy seguro de que, con el apoyo de la nueva responsable de la cuenta, seguiremos avanzando hacia los objetivos acordados.

A continuación, dejo el estado y los próximos pasos por responsable.

### Why

The rewrite keeps the warmth of the original while making the handover clearer and more professional.

## Example 5 — Dutch administrative email: light correction only

### Situation

The user wants to ask whether special coffee filters are required.

### Rough draft

Weet je of er een speciale koffee filters voor de koffeemachine nodig is?

Als ja, dan kan je mischien die kopen of laat me weten welke sort en ik kan het kopen.

Die zijn bijna op.

### Improved

Hi [Name],

Weet je of er speciale koffiefilters nodig zijn voor de koffiemachine?

Zo ja, zou jij die misschien kunnen kopen? Anders kun je me ook laten weten welk type we nodig hebben, dan kan ik ze zelf halen.

De filters zijn bijna op.

Groeten,
[Name]

### Why

The structure was already sufficient. Only grammar and natural Dutch phrasing needed improvement.

## Example 6 — Formal reimbursement request: stronger restructuring

### Situation

The user needs a written request for reimbursement of unauthorized debits.

### Rough notes

According to our phone call I want the amounts taken from my account without authorization returned.
Case number 000000.
Dates and amounts.
They can call another contact if needed.

### Improved structure

Recipient details

Subject: Request for reimbursement of unauthorized debits

Dear [Name],

As discussed during our telephone conversation, I formally request reimbursement of the amounts debited from my account without my authorization.

The request is registered under case number [reference].

The relevant debits are:

[date]: [amount]  
[date]: [amount]  
[date]: [amount]

Total requested: [total]

I did not authorize these debits and therefore request reimbursement of the full amount.

Please confirm receipt of this request in writing, together with the next steps and expected reimbursement timeline.

If telephone contact is required, you may contact [authorized contact] at [number].

Kind regards,
[Name]

### Why

This is more than proofreading. Formal administrative communication benefits from a clear factual record, explicit request, reference number, amounts, and requested confirmation.

## Example 7 — Results communication: lead with the metric and implication

### Situation

A manager needs to communicate an improvement in conversion to senior stakeholders.

### Rough draft

Conversion performed better this month and we saw a good improvement. The team has been working on the checkout changes and it seems to be going well.

### Weak rewrite

Conversion performance improved significantly following the optimization initiatives implemented by the team.

### Improved

Conversion increased from 4.2% to 4.8% this month (+0.6 pp / +14%), exceeding the 4.5% target.

The improvement followed the recent checkout changes. We should continue monitoring the metric over the next period before concluding that the uplift is sustained.

### Why

The result comes first and is supported by specific evidence.

The message distinguishes the observed result from the interpretation of what caused it and avoids overstating causality.

For an operational analytics team, more detail about segments, sample size, experiment methodology, or statistical confidence may be appropriate.

For a Board or executive audience, the result, implication, and material next step may be sufficient.

---

## General lesson from the examples

Match the intervention to the problem.

Do not turn a simple practical message into a formal executive communication merely because a longer version could be written.

Conversely, do not preserve rough notes when the recipient needs a formal, logically complete record.

Use data when it improves the recipient's understanding or decision-making, not simply because metrics are available.
